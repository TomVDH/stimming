# fxlab/

Workbench for the shared shader bundles (`docs/plans/shared-shaders.md`). `noindex`.

- "All effects" stacks every bundle on one window scene, each on a switch with its sliders.
- A new bundle in `shared/` gets a card in the stack.
- The page leads with the picture. Controls stay out of its way.
- "Check a copy" runs a toy's own copy (A) against the module (B), raw WebGL2 or a three.js `ShaderMaterial`, with a difference readout.
- A toy adopts a bundle in its own chat, never from here.
