# menulab/

Workbench for other ways to do the main menu. `noindex`. It changes nothing on the menu.

- The live menu runs in a frame and shows twelve toys, with a Re-roll (key R). A roll never repeats a toy before the rest had a turn.
- Concepts on the switch (`index.html`): Dozen (default), Clock, Dial, Drum. Git history keeps the cut ones.
- Signal Intro menu workbench (`signal.html`): 4 kept (`dial`, `strip`, `rail`, `console`) + 5 new (`magnify`, `orbit`, `folio`, `prism`, `reel`) + 5 parked (`oblique`, `arc`, `drift`, `type`, `corner`, under the Parked toggle). Compact fuzzy masked WebGL2 CRT, live full-colour SVG icons with Intro flash-box-to-colour entrance, Bar v3 / Gate word proportions, `shared/access.js` flags/tech, and a clean All 73 / Series glass sheet.
- Dial and Drum move with GSAP from the menu in the frame.
- A menu concept must stay usable first.
