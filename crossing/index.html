Crossing button

A tick for red, a rattle for green

Many crossing buttons in Belgium and the Netherlands carry a ticker for people who cannot see the lights. On red it ticks slowly, so a blind pedestrian can find the pole by ear. On green it rattles fast, and on many boxes a small arrow shakes under the hand. The Dutch name for it is the rateltikker: the rattle ticker.

The lamp that lights when you press says only that your request is in. A real box counts one press and ignores the rest, and some lights run on a fixed timer anyway. This box keeps count.
