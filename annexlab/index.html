Ruitenbreker /ˈrœytəˌbreːkər/

Shards bring luck, supposedly

Scherven brengen geluk, zeggen ze

No. 13 Lab shelf Sound

How it works

Tap the pane and it cracks from where you hit it. Each crack runs until it meets another crack or the frame.

Hit near a crack and the pane gives way. The shards fall, catch the light and settle on the sill until you sweep them off.
