Why glass breaks in stars

A hit bends the pane away from the blow. The far side stretches, and glass is weak when it stretches. Cracks start there and run out from the hit like spokes.

The spokes leave long wedges that still bend. Each wedge snaps across, and those breaks join into rings around the hit. A hammer and a brick draw the same star at different sizes. A bullet is so fast that it punches a small cone before the star starts.

Making of

One pane, a night city and three passes.

Glass is a three.js scene: one pane in a rounded frame, a room map for the reflections and a night city behind it. There are nine kinds of glass, from Window to Rainbow, and three tools: a hammer, a gun and a brick.

The shot is a Tone.js sound in a small room. It stays silent until you turn the sound on.
