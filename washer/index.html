Pressure washer

Clean lines

A pressure washer drives water through a tiny nozzle at a hundred to two hundred times the pressure of the air. The tip sets the shape of the jet. A red tip makes a pencil-thin jet that cuts hard; a white tip opens it into a wide, gentle fan. Yellow and green sit between them.

Some artists clean pictures into dirt instead of painting them on. In 2006, Alexandre Orion wiped hundreds of skulls into the soot of a road tunnel in São Paulo. The city could find no law against cleaning, so it washed the tunnel instead.
