## 1. Spillway

- **What it is.** A concrete dam in a narrow gorge. A full reservoir stands behind it. Below it, a plunge pool and wet rock walls.
- **The hand.** Drag to open the gates, one or all. The water goes from a trickle to a roar. Mist climbs the gorge walls and hangs. Close the gates and the face drains and drips.
- **Purpose.** Group C. A big machine that holds back a big force, and the hand lets it go. The scale and the sound are the reward. One gesture gives a long, heavy result.
- **Not like.** Railgun and Window seat are sky and light. Lantern lake and Canal at dusk are still water. Calving is one event in nature. Spillway is a steady flow that the hand controls, made by people.
- **Open.** 3D or flat, and the presentation. Day or night? Gates by drag on the dam, or by a lever?

## 2. Deep vent

- **What it is.** Black water at the sea floor. A hydrothermal vent puts out a dark plume. Marine snow falls through the dark.
- **The hand.** The pointer is a lure light. Where it goes, the dark opens: the chimney, the minerals, the plume, and maybe life near the vent.
- **Purpose.** Group C. Light as the gesture. The scene is dark until the hand shows it. It is calm and a little strange.
- **Not like.** Sonar is a display, not a scene. Cloud chamber and Caustic pool are lit all the time. Deep vent is the only toy where the hand carries the only light.
- **Open.** 3D or flat, and the presentation. Life at the vent (tube worms, shrimp), or only rock and smoke?

## 3. Observatory

- **What it is.** An old observatory dome at night, on a hill, with a clear sky full of stars.
- **The hand.** Tap a star. The dome turns, the slit opens and the telescope slews to the star. Maybe a view through the eyepiece.
- **Purpose.** Group C. A slow machine that does what you ask. The joy is in the heavy moving parts and the wait for them.
- **Not like.** Star trails is the sky only, in long exposure. Gear train and Music box are mechanisms with no scene. Observatory joins a mechanism to a night sky.
- **Open.** 3D or flat, and the presentation. Inside the dome or outside? Real, named stars or made-up stars?

## 4. Rain stick

- **What it is.** A long glass tube full of beads and a spiral of pins, on a dark stage.
- **The hand.** Drag turns the tube. The beads rush off the end, then trickle down through the pins. Every hit clicks and lights its pin. A tap gives a shake.
- **Purpose.** Group D. One gesture gives 10 to 15 s of sound and light. The sound is the toy: rain from a stick.
- **Not like.** String harp and Music box need a gesture for each note. Pin art pushes pins; it does not play them. Rain stick lets gravity play a long tail after one turn.
- **Open.** 3D or flat, and the presentation. The plan has a dark stage with the tube only; Tom picked an icon with a seesaw stand (D). Does the stand go in the toy?

## 5. Calm jar

- **What it is.** The inside of a jar of thick, clear liquid full of glitter.
- **The hand.** Drag to swirl. The flakes lift, tumble and flash. Then they settle slowly, big flakes first and fine dust last, in layers.
- **Purpose.** Group D. A calm-down toy. The slow settle is the point: you watch it come to rest.
- **Not like.** Snow globe is a scene in a dome, with snow. Calm jar has no dome, no scene and no snow. Its subject is the optics of the glitter and the sort by size. Plan question F1 asks if this difference is enough.
- **Open.** 3D or flat, and the presentation. F1: is it far enough from Snow globe?

## 6. BZ reaction

- **What it is.** A petri dish of the Belousov-Zhabotinsky reaction on a lightbox. Orange liquid; blue fronts.
- **The hand.** Tap to seed a target: rings pulse out. Drag across a front to break it into a pair of spirals. Two fronts that meet wipe each other out.
- **Purpose.** Group A. A real chemical clock that you can draw with. It is small (size S) and lifts Turing skin's sim.
- **Not like.** Turing skin makes still patterns that grow. BZ makes waves that move and never stop. Chladni plate needs sound; BZ needs only a touch.
- **Open.** 3D or flat, and the presentation. Real BZ colours (orange to blue) or a free palette?

## 7. Calving

- **What it is.** A fjord with a glacier wall. Dark mountains, brash ice, calm black-green water.
- **The hand.** Tap the ice face. A crack runs, a tower of ice leans and falls, the splash goes up, and a slow surge rolls across the fjord to the camera. Drag pans along the face.
- **Purpose.** Group C. Scale and slowness. A huge thing falls at real speed, and the result rolls on for a long time.
- **Not like.** Railgun is sky and cloud. Tornado alley is a storm over land. Calving is one huge event in ice and water, with a long tail after it. It is the largest build (XXXL).
- **Open.** 3D or flat, and the presentation. Which XXXL comes first: Calving or Tornado alley? Build after Spillway, so its water and mist carry over?

## 8. Aquarium

- **What it is.** A planted fish tank: sand, plants, clear water, a few bright fish.
- **The hand.** Tap to drop food: the fish rise to it. Tap the glass: they scatter, then calm down and come back.
- **Purpose.** Group F. A quiet thing to keep on the desk and look at. Small animals that react to you.
- **Not like.** Terrarium is plants in a sealed jar, with no animals. Murmuration is a flock in the sky. Caustic pool is light on water. Jellyfish tank (idea 80) is near; Aquarium has fish with behaviour and a tank you look into.
- **Open.** 3D or flat, and the presentation. Fresh water or salt water? Real species or made-up fish? A bean?

## 9. Iodine clock

- **What it is.** A lab beaker. Two clear liquids mix. After a set delay, the whole beaker snaps to ink black.
- **The hand.** Pour one liquid into the other. A slider sets the delay. Then you wait, and the snap comes at once.
- **Purpose.** Group A. A toy about time: the wait and the surprise. It is a classic school demo.
- **Not like.** Hot ice and Flame test change when you touch them. Ink Water spreads colour slowly. Iodine clock changes on its own, all at once, after a delay you set.
- **Open.** 3D or flat, and the presentation. One beaker, or two that pour? Show a timer? A bean?

## 10. Electrolysis

- **What it is.** A glass cell of water with an indicator, and two electrodes.
- **The hand.** Switch the current on. Gas streams off both electrodes, twice as much at minus. Colour clouds bloom at each pole: red at plus, violet at minus. Move a rod or turn the voltage to change the rate.
- **Purpose.** Group A. Cause and effect with a dial. Invisible chemistry made into colour and bubbles.
- **Not like.** Ink Water and Marbling spread dye by hand. Bubble wand blows bubbles. Electrolysis makes both from a current, and the colours tell acid from base.
- **Open.** 3D or flat, and the presentation. Which indicator (universal or red cabbage)? A battery in view? A bean?
