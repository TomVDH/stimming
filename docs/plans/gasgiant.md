# Gas giant dive (`gasgiant`) plan

Status: active (Tom approved 2026-11-23; defaults Q1 to Q5 hold; the dive now runs to 640 km) · Bean: `stimming-vxno`

Idea 367, group C, size XXXL (Tom's picks, 2026-11-23): "Fall from orbit past the rings into the cloud bands. A great storm turns, lightning flashes deep down, and the light fails as the pressure builds."
Tom approved the plan on 2026-11-23. The MVP is in the build.

## Context

Tom wants four parade pieces at XXL and up. This one is the deepest fall on the shelf: a cinematic scene at Railgun scale, cut first to one finished hero moment in the Lab, as Volcano was.
The full toy runs from orbit to the dark. The MVP is the last third: the cloud tops to the dark, where the look is hardest and the payoff is largest.

## 3. The MVP cut: "The plunge" (stages 5 and 6)

One hero moment, finished to high fidelity, ships first to the Lab.

1. **First frame:** 260 km over the 1 bar level, above the haze, looking 35 degrees down. The deck runs to a curved horizon in its bands. The great storm fills the lower right: its collar and spiral, its far side past the horizon. The rings stand as an arch across the sky, seen from their unlit side. Their shadow lies in stripes on the deck ahead.
2. **Hold (or Space):** you dive. The fall gains speed, the frame shakes a little, the wind roar grows. The deck rises; the storm's collar rises past the frame's edge; the cloud detail grows with each km.
3. **Into the decks:** a white-out in the ammonia deck at 0.7 bar; then the brown deck at 2 bar, the light amber; then the water deck at 5 bar, the light deep red. Lightning flashes below and lights the cloud from inside; its thunder comes late.
4. **The dark:** at 20 bar the sun is gone. Only the flashes show the cloud, each a soft blue-white patch.
5. **Release:** the fall eases to a hang over 3 s. You stay at that depth; the clouds drift by; lightning goes on.
6. **Rise:** drag the Depth slider up; you rise back through the decks to the top. Reset does the same at once.

## 4. Tech

### Renderer

Raw WebGL2 on `shared/gl.js` (stack tier 3, the Environment type in spirit). Nothing in the MVP is a mesh: a sphere, a ring plane and a cloud volume, all analytic or marched. three.js would add load and nothing else. Stage 3's ring blocks can be instanced points in raw GL later.
Not `shared/environment.js`: it is built for ground scenes with water and glass.

### Passes (one output each, for the D3D11 link)

| Pass | Size | Holds | When |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Map | 1024 x 512, RGBA16F | Band colour, cover, top height, storm vorticity, in a frame that follows the eye. Flow-map advection, two phases cross-faded, so the shear never winds up. | 30 fps or less |
| 2. Sky | full | Space, the sun, the rings (radial profile as a 1D texture, light that passes through from the unlit side, the planet's shadow), the limb haze (`shared/air.js` with hydrogen values) | On a camera move |
| 3. March | half (a third on rung 2) | Clouds: rgb light, a transmittance. Sun light with Fire's smoke shading, the ring shadow (one ray to the ring plane, analytic), sky light, lightning | Each frame |
| 4. Composite | full | Upsample (depth-aware 4 taps), the sky under the clouds, the in-medium fog from the light table | Each frame |
| 5. Post | full | Bloom (`shared/glow.js`), grade and grain (`shared/grade.js`), output (`shared/output.js`) | Each frame |

The light table: a 1D bake (256 rows by altitude) of sun transmittance and fog colour through the decks, rgb. It makes the light fail: blue dies first (the brown chromophores take it), then green, then red. A slow auto-exposure chases it, so the dark arrives in steps and is final.

### The cloud volume

- **Template:** copy `volcano/clouds.js` `marchSrc` (lines 154 to 235) and `bakeNoise` (42 to 62), inside `/* ---------- K: cloud march ---------- */` marks. Never import from another toy.
- **Shading:** Fire's smoke (`shared/fire.js:500-577`), as ported in `volcano/plume.js`: Henyey-Greenstein phase times 4 pi, `beer = exp(-tau)`, multiple scatter `0.5 exp(-0.25 tau)`, powder `1 - exp(-2 tau)`, a shadow ambient. Two-lobe phase from `shared/clouds.js` (`clPhase`), imported as is.
- **Shape:** the map gives the large form; a 64 cubed tiling billow noise gives the detail. Octaves fade by pixel footprint, so one march serves 260 km up and 2 km away.
- **Filaments:** Juno's look is folded streaks, not puffs. The detail noise is stretched along the local flow (the map's velocity) and curl-warped, so belts read as combed fibres.
- **Storm:** a Rankine vortex in the map. The spiral turns as rings of rigid bodies, each at its own rate, as Tornado alley's onion sheaths do; nothing winds up. Its top stands 8 km over the deck, red.
- **Steps:** distance-growing steps, an empty-space skip, a 5-step bisect at the deck entry (from volcano). Inside a deck, the march covers 40 km; the light table fogs the rest.
- **Lightning:** Railgun's burst timing (`railgun/src/clouds/lightning.ts:12-16`: 1 to 5 strokes, log-normal brightness), copied as JS. In the march, up to 4 flash points: `(1.8 gauss + 0.5 exp)` reach, no shadow, as in volcano and Railgun storms. Colour blue-white.

### D3D11 link rules (from Car wash, Cauldron, Tornado)

1. One output per program. Volcano and Jet hover use two; this toy does not.
2. Rolled loops with uniform bounds; `textureLod(..., 0.0)` in every loop.
3. One call site per heavy function; one `return`; every variable set on every path.
4. `pruneGLSL()` from `shared/glsl-prune.js` on each pass; `linked()` for parallel compile.
5. Clamp every `pow()` base. Budget: each pass links in 3 s or less on D3D11; first frame in 6 s or less. `shared/loadwatch.js` records the load steps.

## 5. Sound, bar and rules

### Bar v3

| Control | Content |
|---|---|
| Action | Dive (hold), key Space |
| Slider | Depth: drag up to rise, down to sink. It shows where you are. |
| Settings | Quality (shared), Reset (back to the cloud tops) |
| How | Hold to dive · drag to look · Depth to rise · Space |

`initPage({ id, bar })` (Bar v3 is the default). `detail()` from `shared/detail.js` for the ladder.

### Sound (Web Audio through `audioKit`, muted by default)

| Sound | When |
|---|---|
| Wind roar, filtered noise | Grows with the fall speed; the low-pass falls as the pressure rises, so the air sounds thicker |
| Buffet thumps | Random, with the dive |
| Pressure hum | A low tone that climbs slowly with depth |
| Thunder | Late by distance at 1.2 km/s, the speed of sound in hydrogen at 5 bar. Scheduled on the audio clock; mute and a hidden tab cancel it |

### Frame loop and rules

1. Lively while the hand holds, the fall eases, or a flash runs.
2. Drifting at 30 fps while the bands shear and the storm turns.
3. Asleep 60 s after the last input; motion eases to a stop over 2 s. Hidden: the loop and the sim clock stop at once.
4. Reduced motion: no shake, no drift. Flashes softer and fewer. At all times, flashes stay at 3 a second or fewer.
5. Ladder (`detail.js`, three rungs): pixel ratio to 1; march buffer to a third; steps and octaves down.

## 6. Cost, risks, bean, questions

### Cost

### Risks

| Risk | Answer |
|---|---|
| One volume from 260 km up to 2 km away looks flat far or blurred near | Octaves by pixel footprint; Q4 lets the start move lower |
| Noise puffs instead of Juno's filaments | Detail stretched along the flow; check against PIA22946 at each look pass |
| The march does not link on D3D11 | One output, five small passes, a per-pass link timer from day one |
| Inside a deck the whole frame marches dense medium | A 40 km march window plus the fog table beyond it |
| Flashes harm a photosensitive viewer | 3 a second cap; soft patches, not full-frame white |
