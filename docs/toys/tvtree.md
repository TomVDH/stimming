## Idea

A tree hung with old TV sets on a night stage, each set on its own channel. Click a set to change its channel, drag to move it, hold to switch it on and off; when the sets agree, one big picture spans the whole tree.

## Rules

- `tvtree/channels.js` holds the channel library: 155 programmes and Static, 156 channels in all (`NAMES`, `NCH`).
- Programmes sit in groups of 25, one shader per group (`GROUP`, `GROUPS`). Six groups are full; the seventh holds five.
- Static is the last channel. It lives in every group and draws with the first.
- `tvtree/channels.html` is the live contact sheet. The Settings sheet links it.
- `tvtree/lab.html` is the older four-tree lab, `noindex`.
