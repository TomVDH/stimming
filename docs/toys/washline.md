## Idea

A pulley line on a cliff top over a bay of sea fog at dawn. You hang the washing yourself: add a piece, reel it out, slide the Sun until the cloth glows, and pull pegs until a piece flies away.

## Rules

- Every outside asset (for example CC0 textures) needs Tom's yes before the download, and a row in `washline/CREDITS.md`.

## Direction

- One place: a cliff top in sea fog at sunrise.
- One camera, composed like a still, about 1.3 m up and under the line. It looks east over the bay.
- The line and the backlit washing are the subject. The sun rises behind the washing.
- The line always contrasts with what is behind it and sits close enough to read.
- Light, air and fog carry the depth: layers pale with distance. Architecture stays close and few.
- Hero props are modelled with care: bevels, wear, real proportions.
- Out: stylised skies, sticker clouds, anime cel shading, boxy mid-distance buildings, many small instanced props, the Iso view.
