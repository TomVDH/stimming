## Idea

A diorama of East Vancouver in Toy-town 3D: a house, its yard, the back lane and a block of Commercial Drive at East 4th Avenue. Poke everything; nearly every object reacts.

## Rules

- Every asset download needs Tom's yes first. Before each batch, list the file, the source, the size and the licence.
- Every asset gets a row in `eastvan/CREDITS.md`: file, source URL, author, licence, date.
- City data on screen needs its licence credit on the page.
- Build on `eastvan/index.html`, `town.js`, `lens.js`, `lot.js` and `modules/`. The looks lab (`looks.html`, `looks-lot.js`) stays frozen.
- `eastvan/postcard/` is a separate study. Its own session works on it.

## Direction

- Toy-town: true 3D on a plinth, flat colour, an ink edge line. Full force.
- Paper theatre and Print poster wait for later.
- Smooth and stylistically interesting.
- The main verb is poke: nearly every object reacts.
- The house and the alley are archetypes from real East Van parts. One block is real: Commercial Drive at East 4th Avenue.
- Public assets only: open city data, CC0 models, CC0 textures and sound.
