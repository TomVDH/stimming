## Idea

A harbour in fog, seen from 7 m over the water through the mouth of the breakwater. Sound the horn, and a ship answers out of the fog and creeps in through the mouth.

## Rules

- The ship is the KS Vlootzak, drawn from two flat renders: `vlootzak-side.webp` and `vlootzak-end.webp`.
- The model (Havoc's `bulker.glb`) never ships: its source licence is unknown. Draw from its measures.
- `npm run check` type-checks `foghorn/main.js`.

## Direction

- Big things move at their real speed or slower. Never compress a slow event into a quick pass.
- Start from the real speed: a ship in harbour makes 2 to 3 m/s. The Vlootzak creeps in at under five knots.
- If the wait is too long, start the ship nearer. Do not speed it up.
