Morpher

Shapes as distances

Every pixel asks one question: how far am I from the edge? Inside a shape the answer is negative, outside it is positive, and the outline is where it reaches zero.

The blobs melt into each other because their distances are blended with a smooth minimum. Where two shapes come close, the blend rounds the gap into a bridge. Blend sets how wide that bridge can grow.
