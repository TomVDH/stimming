East Van Alley

The dusk commute

Every evening, crows from all over Vancouver fly east to one roost by Still Creek, in Burnaby. Thousands of them use the same routes. On the way they stop on the power lines in the back lanes of East Van, a few hundred to a block, and talk.

A sudden noise splits the stream. One startled bird alarms its neighbours, and the alarm runs through the flock faster than any one bird can fly. Then the gap closes and the birds fly on.
