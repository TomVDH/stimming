Ferrofluid, round 1 under its own name; spike 6 holds its earlier A and D. A: the hedgehog, a dome of glossy black spikes in its puddle. D: a nickel magnet cube with the fluid bristling on its top, tallest at the corners. In both the pointer is the toy's magnet glow: the spikes lean and stretch toward it, and a bead lifts to it.
