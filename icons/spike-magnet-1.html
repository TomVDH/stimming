Icon spike: Magnet balls 1

Magnet balls, round 1. Tom kept the Current icon; this round offers a new A and D. A: a square pyramid of fourteen polished balls, each with a copper pole and a blue pole and the dark cut between them, as the toy draws them. D: the same pyramid on a block of the toy's slate table, the field glowing warm and cool on its top. The pointer is a magnet: every pole turns to it, and the top ball lifts off toward it.
