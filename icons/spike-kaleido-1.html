Icon spike: Kaleidoscope 1

Kaleidoscope, round 1 under its own name; spike 7 held its earlier A and D, both cut. A: the kaleidoscope itself, a cobalt and brass collector piece, its wide bead cell turned toward us; on the pointer the cell turns and the beads tumble. D: the same scope lying on a walnut block, its cell over the near corner, spare beads beside it.
