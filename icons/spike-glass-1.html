Glass, round 1 of the every-toy pass. Tom kept Current (the shaded pane redo). A: a thick sheet of float glass at a three-quarter turn, a spider-web fracture round a crushed white point, green cut edges in every crack. D: the same pane standing in a slot of the toy's sea-green sill, which is the block. On both, the pointer knocks shards out; they fall and lie in front; leave rewinds them, as the toy does.
