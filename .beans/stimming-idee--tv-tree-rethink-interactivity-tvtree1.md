## Design: Pass the picture (Tom picked B, 2026-11-16)

Why: four alike sets start the big picture, but today only luck finds it. Make it the game.

Gestures
- Drag a set: it swings, as now. Let go over another set: the picture jumps across, a small spark runs between them, and the target takes the channel.
- Tap a set: next channel. Hold: power off. Both stay.
- Four alike start the big picture (exists today); two or three ring a rising tone. Threads between alike sets were built and cut (Tom, 2026-11-16: "get rid").
- Tap the lantern at the trunk foot: it lights. L stays.

Bar (Bar v2)
- Actions: Shuffle (S), Eyes (E), Lights out (O). Tune goes; T and the timed big picture stay.
- Lights out: every set off in a wave from the trunk; with all off, it reads Lights on. A dark tree draws no frames.
- Strip: click change channel, drag swing or pass, hold power off, scroll zoom.
- Settings: Quality and the Channels link only. Power and Lantern rows go. P stays as a hidden key.

Approaches considered: A (things on the tree), C (bedtime slider). B won: it turns the hidden goal into play and keeps every gesture.

Build order, one look each: 1 pass, 2 thread, 3 Lights out, lantern tap, bar.

## Summary of Changes

- Pass the picture: a set let go over another set sends its picture across on a warm spark with a soft halo. The target lights first; a dark target switches on. C lifts and drops by keyboard.
- Bar: Shuffle (S), Eyes (E), Lights out (O). Lights out reads Lights on while every set is dark. Tune leaves the bar; T, P and L stay as keys.
- Lantern: always drawn, dark glass when unlit; a tap lights it. Its choice keeps the old Settings key, stimming.tvtree.lantern.
- Settings: Channels and Quality only. Strip: drag swing or pass.
- Threads between alike sets: built and cut by Tom.
