Tom, 2026-11-24: "interacting binary". Two stars so close that one pours gas onto the other: a bright stream arcs across, a thick disc of gas wraps the receiving star, and the pair eclipse each other as they orbit. Beta Lyrae is the classic (a 12.9-day orbit); Algol is the famous eclipser.
