## Plan

- Cyclone void -262 to -470 km in place of the gyre, salt deck, lightning nest and stairs. The cyclone is a pit: a turning ring round a funnel, 560 km across at the rim, 140 km at the throat, which runs on through the floor into the dark. The eye falls down the pit 40 km off its axis; the glide slows to 0.6 there (about 70 s held).
- Bent lightning in the pit's walls, drawn over the march and the bake.
- Shear -470 to -540 and Crush -540 to -640; the hull fails at about 4:10.
- Signs of life by depth: light motes (mushball fall), a slick on the glass (water deck), gasbags (shelves), a vast manta among the columns (nave), shapes in the bolt light (pit).
- Tom's horror picks: wrong light (god rays rise from below), the murmuration, glyphs in the cracks.
