Kintsugi

Mended with gold

Kintsugi is the Japanese craft of mending broken pottery. The pieces are glued with urushi, the sap of the lacquer tree, built up in thin coats that cure for weeks, and the last coat is dusted with powdered gold. A chip that is lost is filled with lacquer and covered in gold too. The story goes that it began in the fifteenth century, when a tea bowl came back from a repair held together with ugly metal staples.

The repair does not hide the break. Here a tap is the blow: cracks run out from where you strike, and the bowl falls apart on the cloth. Then the pieces come back together and gold runs from the point of the blow along every seam. Each break takes new lines and the old gold stays, so no two bowls end the same.
