# serieslab/

Workbench for series icons. `noindex`. The standard is in `docs/menu.md` (Series).

- `shots.html`: every shot, with a large stage and tilt, frame, line and Vistas colour knobs.
- `blocks.html`: single-block icons kept for later.
- `places`, `systems`, `funk` and `iso` are rejected directions. Do not propose neutral geometric or line icon systems.
- A series icon draws the idea of the series, never one of its toys.
