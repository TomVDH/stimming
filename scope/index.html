Oscilloscope

Two channels, one beam

In X–Y mode an oscilloscope stops drawing time. The left channel pushes the beam across and the right channel pushes it up, so a stereo signal draws a shape. A circle is two sine waves a quarter turn apart.

In Pen, your stroke is the signal: the beam runs it out and back, and a closed loop just goes round. Pitch sets the laps per second. The phosphor burns hardest where the beam slows, so corners flare. In Knot, two pure tones tie the shape; their ratio picks the knot, and a slight detune rolls it.
