Car wash

Open all night

It stands off a two-lane road in the desert: a low stucco building, a canopy, a pylon with an arrow and a ring of bulbs, most of them dead. It went up some time in the fifties. The county has no owner on file, the power bill is paid in cash, and the booth has been empty for as long as anyone has driven past.

The sign says it never closes, and as far as anyone knows, it never has. Leave the car in neutral, hands off the wheel, and the chain pulls it through at a third of a metre a second: soak, suds, cloth, brushes, rinse, wax, the blower. Most people come out clean.
