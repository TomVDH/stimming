# Fog horn: credits

`vlootzak-side.webp` and `vlootzak-end.webp` are two flat renders of the KS Vlootzak.
The model is Havoc's bulk carrier (`lab/ocean/assets/bulker.glb`, from "MV Cymona Eagle").
Havoc's own credits list that source's licence as unknown.
Tom approved shipping the two renders on 2026-11-10. The model itself does not ship.
The funnel's company mark is painted out.
